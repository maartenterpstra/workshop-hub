Deep Learning in Radiotherapy 2026

A two-day workshop and scientific symposium on deep learning applications in radiotherapy, hosted at UMC Utrecht.

March 5–6, 2026 UMC Utrecht, The Netherlands Auditorium, Q-building
← Back to current edition

About the 2026 edition

The 2026 workshop combined a 1-day educational programme aimed at medical physicists and clinicians, followed by a 1-day scientific symposium of the Radiotherapy Expert group initiative. All presentations were in English and the event was held in person at the UMC Utrecht.

Programme overview

Educational Workshop

Thursday, March 5, 2026
  • Introduction to machine and deep learning
  • Image synthesis
  • Auto-contouring
  • Treatment planning
  • Image registration & contour propagation
  • Large language models
  • Clinical implementation

Scientific Symposium

Friday, March 6, 2026
  • Image synthesis & reconstruction
  • Contouring & registration
  • Clinical predictions
  • Clinical implementation

Venue

UMC Utrecht — Auditorium, Q-building

Department of Radiotherapy, Heidelberglaan 100, 3584 CX Utrecht, The Netherlands.

Reachable from Utrecht Central Station via tram/bus line 12 towards Utrecht Science Park (about 15 minutes).

Organisers
